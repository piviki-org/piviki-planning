- (requirement) R1 (brief Scope, addendum Multi-language): A patron can use every patron-facing screen in Vietnamese or English, and adding another language needs translation work only.
- (requirement) R2 (brief Scope, Who This Serves; addendum Accounts, Phone number): An adult can sign up after ticking "I confirm I am 18 or older"; sign-up asks for no phone number and the system holds no data about children.
- (requirement) R3 (brief Scope, Solution): A patron can browse and search the catalogue and see whether a book is available.
- (requirement) R4 (brief Solution, addendum Queues): A patron can reserve an available book, or join the queue for a book on loan, and sees the request on their account.
- (requirement) R5 (brief Solution): A patron's account shows what they have, what they are waiting for, and when each book is due.
- (requirement) R6 (brief Scope, addendum Queues): A patron can renew a loan online within the renewal rules in force, and a renewal is refused when others are queueing for the book.
- (requirement) R7 (brief Scope, addendum Overdue consequence): A patron with an overdue book cannot reserve until it is returned, and no fine is ever charged.
- (requirement) R8 (brief Solution, Scope): A patron receives an email when a reserved book is ready for pickup.
- (requirement) R9 (addendum Reminders): A patron receives an email 5, 3 and 1 days before a loan's due date.
- (requirement) R10 (addendum Reminders): A patron with an overdue book receives one email a week until it is returned.
- (requirement) R11 (brief Solution, Scope): A librarian can add and edit a book by fast manual entry, without any online metadata existing for it.
- (requirement) R12 (brief Scope, Solution): A librarian adding a book can fill its record by ISBN lookup when a record exists.
- (requirement) R13 (brief Scope, addendum Cover-scan cataloguing script): A script turns scanned covers into catalogue records with Vietnamese diacritics intact, and a librarian reviews every record before it is imported in bulk.
- (requirement) R14 (brief Solution): A librarian can move a reservation through Reserve, Ready for pickup, Picked up (the loan starts) and Returned, and can see current loans and queues at a glance.
- (requirement) R15 (addendum Uncollected reservations): A reservation is flagged 7 days after it is marked ready for pickup, and a librarian can then expire it by hand; nothing expires automatically.
- (requirement) R16 (brief Scope): The pickup address is shown only to a patron with a confirmed pickup and never appears on the public site.
- (requirement) R17 (brief Scope, addendum Donations): A librarian can record a copy as donated with a date and an optional donor, and the donor link can be removed without deleting the copy or its history.
- (requirement) R18 (addendum Phone number): A librarian can add a patron's phone number at their first pickup.
- (requirement) R19 (brief Scope, addendum GDPR, Design notes): A patron can read a privacy notice that states the retention period and covers the phone number and possible future donation benefits, and can delete their account.
- (requirement) R20 (brief Decided later): Loan period, maximum books per patron, and renewal rules and limits work as placeholder values that the stakeholders' decisions can replace, without blocking launch.
- (requirement) R21 (brief Milestones): The patron website replaces the coming-soon page at piviki.fi at launch.
- (constraint) Never cut: reservations, the queue, the pickup loop, check-in, the ready-for-pickup email and the overdue block (brief Milestones).
- (constraint) If a milestone slips, cut in this order: ISBN lookup (R12), then self-renewal (R6), then due-date reminders (R9), then the English interface while multi-language support stays (R1); the overdue notice (R10) stays (brief Milestones).
- (constraint) Milestones are internal targets: catalogue in use with the 300 books imported by end of November 2026, patron website live by mid-December 2026, first real loan by end of December 2026; the library opens when the software is ready (brief Milestones).
- (constraint) A two-person part-time team builds and runs this, which is why the cut order exists (brief Risks).
- (constraint) Pickup and drop-off happen at a librarian's home, and pickup times are arranged in the community group chat, outside the system (brief Solution, addendum Pickup and drop-off).
- (constraint) The data model must not rule out running more than one library, and more languages must remain translation work (brief Scope, addendum Multi-library, Multi-language).
- (constraint) The code and the data belong to the community, not to a subscription service (brief What Makes This Different).
- (constraint) Vietnamese is the first language of the experience, in an interface the team controls end to end (brief What Makes This Different).
- (constraint) Email goes through a transactional email service on the library's own domain; a personal Gmail account and Proton are rejected (addendum Email).
- (constraint) Data is minimal, retention periods are stated, each hosting or SaaS provider has a written data processing agreement, and a breach can be notified within 72 hours (addendum GDPR).
- (constraint) Donor data, when kept, is donor ID, copy ID and date only, with no free-text names or contact details; only patrons with accounts can be credited, and walk-in donors are anonymous; account deletion anonymises the link (addendum Design notes).
- (constraint) A donor's name is never shown publicly without a separate opt-in (addendum Design notes).
- (assumption) The catalogue can be browsed and searched without an account, since the brief speaks of a public site and families need to see the collection.
- (assumption) Queues run first come, first served, and an expired reservation or a returned book passes to the next patron in line.
- (assumption) Catalogue search finds Vietnamese titles whether or not the parent types the diacritics.
- (assumption) Emails go out in the language the patron chose, and the librarian tool is not translated for the MVP.
- (assumption) The public catalogue shows cover images, since the addendum rates the copyright risk as low for a non-profit catalogue.
