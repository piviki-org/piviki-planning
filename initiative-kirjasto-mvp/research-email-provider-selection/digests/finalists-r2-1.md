## Leads
- Read the Lettermint Return-Path record name and value from the dashboard, and check whether Lettermint adds a "List-Unsubscribe" or footer header to transactional mail.
- Lettermint trust centre at trust.lettermint.co: check the ISO 27001 certificate and DPA PDF.
- Scaleway TEM Specific Conditions v04-2026 PDF (www-uploads.scaleway.com/Specific_Conditions_Transactionnal_Email_Services_v042026_3f5487a93c.pdf) and the DPA at scaleway.com/en/contracts. Open them in a browser, since the fetcher got 403.
- Scaleway subprocessor list at scaleway.com/en/subprocessorlist, for payment and support processors.
- Independent comparison listings: european-alternatives.eu (Scaleway TEM), euro-stack.com (both vendors), eualternative.eu.
