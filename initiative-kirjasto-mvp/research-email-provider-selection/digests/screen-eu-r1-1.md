## Scaleway Transactional Email (TEM)

- Essential plan: pay as you go, 300 free emails/month, then €0.25 per 1,000. Up to 5 domains and 1 webhook per domain. Scale plan: fixed monthly fee, 100K emails, then €0.20 per 1,000, with a dedicated IP. | https://github.com/scaleway/docs-content/blob/main/pages/transactional-email/concepts.mdx ; .../how-to/manage-tem-plans.mdx | Scaleway | undated (repo current 2026-09) | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class pricing
- Billing covers only emails above the free allowance (FAQ example: 305 sent means 5 billed). Sending via API or SMTP is available on Essential. | https://github.com/scaleway/docs-content/blob/main/pages/transactional-email/faq.mdx | Scaleway | validated 2025-09-24 | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class pricing/capability
- The default quota is 10,000 emails/month. "New users may face stricter initial limits". To raise quotas you need a validated payment method and identity, plus a Support request. Hourly quota was removed 2023-12-01. Service runs only in the fr-par region, and the recursive SPF include directive is not supported. | https://github.com/scaleway/docs-content/blob/main/pages/transactional-email/reference-content/tem-capabilities-and-limits.mdx | Scaleway | validated 2025-10-29 | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class capability
- DNS records: SPF `v=spf1 include:_spf.tem.scaleway.com -all` (or add the include to an existing record), a DKIM TXT record whose name and value come from the console, DMARC `_dmarc.<domain>` e.g. `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:...`, and a recommended MX (own, or Scaleway's blackhole MX). SPF and DKIM must be in place before sending, followed by a manual verify. Autoconfig is available if DNS is at Scaleway. | https://github.com/scaleway/docs-content/blob/main/pages/transactional-email/how-to/authenticate-domain.mdx ; faq.mdx | Scaleway | undated | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class capability
- The official FAQ says no non-EU sub-processors are used for TEM. The whole stack is managed by Scaleway within the EU, no personal data is transferred outside the EU, so no TIA was needed. The Scaleway DPA (Art. 11 SCCs) is referenced. | https://github.com/scaleway/docs-content/blob/main/pages/transactional-email/faq.mdx | Scaleway | validated 2025-09-24 | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class compliance
- Scaleway is a French company (Iliad group). This is a belief, not verified this run. | - | - | - | - | confidence low | class compliance
- Domains are auto-blocked when the undelivered-email rate is high, based on a reputation score. TEM is for transactional mail only, with an anti-spam policy. | faq.mdx (as above) | Scaleway | - | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class deliverability

## Lettermint

- Lettermint B.V. is incorporated in the Netherlands. Its own infrastructure runs only in Europe, with encrypted backups at a separate provider in Germany. It says email data is processed and stored only in the EU. It holds ISO 27001:2022. | https://lettermint.co/llms.txt ; https://lettermint.co/european-email | Lettermint | undated | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high (self-declared) | class compliance
- Free plan: 300 emails/month, no daily limit, no credit card. Starter €10/mo for 10,000 emails. SMTP relay on all plans including Free. Message history kept 28 days on all plans. Open/click tracking only from Starter up (so not on Free). | https://lettermint.co/pricing | Lettermint | undated | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class pricing
- The paid tiers are Starter €10, Growth €13 and Pro €15/mo, all with 10k emails. "A GDPR Data Processing Agreement applies to every plan." There is a public Art. 28 DPA, and a countersigned copy is available on request. | https://lettermint.co/llms.txt ; https://lettermint.co/dpa | Lettermint | undated | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class pricing/compliance
- Sub-processors (updated 2026-10-29): UpCloud (NL, hosting), OVH (DE, backups) and Postmastery (NL, deliverability monitoring) handle email. Aikido (EU, security) and Mistral (EU, support/abuse) have no email access. No US sub-processors. Logs are kept ≤14 days, with 30 days' notice of new sub-processors. | https://lettermint.co/subprocessors | Lettermint | 2026-10-29 | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class compliance
- Retention: message content and recipient metadata are kept 28 days (not configurable). Analytics events are kept 90 days from 8 Nov 2026 (28 days before then), with HMAC-hashed recipients. Raw tracking IP/UA data is kept 28 days. Content redaction is available per project/route, depending on plan. | https://lettermint.co/docs/platform/emails/data-retention | Lettermint | undated | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class compliance
- Tracking is configured per route. Click tracking rewrites links via t.ltrmnt.com. Opens use an injected pixel. SMTP overrides: `X-LM-Override-Track-Opens: false` and `X-LM-Override-Track-Clicks: false`. The docs suggest keeping transactional routes tracking-free. The default state per route is not stated explicitly, but Free has no tracking. | https://lettermint.co/docs/platform/emails/tracking/introduction | Lettermint | undated | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class capability
- DNS: a subdomain is recommended. You add DKIM as CNAMEs (`lm1._domainkey`, plus a second one for auto-rotation), a Return-Path CNAME (SPF is handled through it, no separate SPF record) and DMARC. First verification is blocked if DMARC has `aspf=s`; it must be `aspf=r`. | https://lettermint.co/docs/platform/domains/introduction | Lettermint | undated | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class capability
- Onboarding gate: an unverified team can only send to team members and test addresses. To lift that, you fill in a compliance form (project description, URL, volume, email types, opt-in, bounce handling) and wait for review. | https://lettermint.co/docs/platform/onboarding/compliance-verification | Lettermint | undated | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class capability
- MFA: passkeys, OAuth, team MFA enforcement, and SSO/SCIM on paid plans. | https://lettermint.co/docs/platform/teams/security | Lettermint | undated | accessed 2026-11-03 | confidence high | class capability
