# Piviki opens

## Description

Privacy notice, usage policy, retention, account deletion and lost copies are live, the GDPR check is answered, the success signals can be read, piviki.fi serves the patron website, and the first real loan is made.

## Outcome

For the community, Piviki opens lawfully at piviki.fi and makes its first real loan.

## Done when

- The privacy notice and usage policy are published, and the Art. 9 question is answered, before sign-up opens.
- A patron deletes their account unless holding a book, and a librarian can delete one and mark the held copies lost, with reservations and affected queue places cancelled.
- The retention period is applied to loan records, and every success signal can be read from the records.
- piviki.fi serves the patron website in place of the coming-soon page.
- A first real loan is recorded in production.

## Boundaries

- Owns touch point: the piviki.fi switch; Facebook and Instagram links stay outside the system.
- Depends on people: the other founder writes the usage policy; stakeholders set the loan values.
- Not here: tenant or donor features beyond the initiative's Boundaries.
